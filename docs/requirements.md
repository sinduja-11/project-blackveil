
# PROJECT BLACKVEIL
## System Requirements Specification

## 1. Introduction

This document defines the functional and non-functional requirements of PROJECT BLACKVEIL.

PROJECT BLACKVEIL is an adaptive security layer for permissioned blockchain networks. It verifies node identities, evaluates behavioural patterns, calculates dynamic risk scores and applies adaptive security responses.

The system also provides an isolated decoy blockchain for investigating suspicious sessions while protecting the real blockchain through independent transaction validation.

## 2. Functional Requirements

Functional requirements describe the operations that the system must perform.

### FR-01: Node Identity Verification

- The system shall maintain registered node identities.
- Each node shall have an associated Ed25519 public key.
- The gateway shall verify digital signatures before accepting requests.
- Invalid signatures shall be recorded as security events.
- Repeated identity verification failures shall increase the session's risk level.

### FR-02: Behavioural Monitoring

- The system shall monitor requests generated by participating nodes.
- It shall maintain behavioural information for each node session.
- Behavioural features shall be calculated using a 60-second sliding window.
- The system shall monitor:
  1. Request rate deviation.
  2. Operation-sequence surprise.
  3. Distinct channels and keys accessed.
  4. Transaction value deviation.
  5. Read-to-write ratio changes.
  6. Source changes, including IP and TLS fingerprint.
  7. Integrity violations, including replayed nonces and invalid signatures.

### FR-03: Dynamic Risk Assessment

- The system shall calculate a risk score between 0 and 100.
- The risk engine shall combine:
  1. Rule-based security checks.
  2. Isolation Forest anomaly detection.
  3. CUSUM-based behavioural drift detection.
- The system shall maintain a smoothed risk score to avoid unnecessary fluctuations.
- The system shall identify and record the three most significant contributing features.
- The system shall use pooled peer-group baselines during cold start until sufficient node-specific observations are available.

### FR-04: Adaptive Response Mechanism

The system shall support four response levels.

| Risk score | Response | Expected behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| 0–29 | Observe | Allow normal operations and log activity |
| 30–54 | Challenge | Require fresh signed challenge-response and tighter rate limits |
| 55–74 | Restrict | Apply read-only mode, transaction value caps and additional endorsement requirements |
| 75–100 | Divert | Redirect eligible suspicious sessions to the isolated decoy environment |

Additional requirements:

- The system shall increase response severity quickly when risk rises.
- It shall decrease response severity only after sustained improvement.
- Diversion shall require corroborating evidence, such as two consecutive high-risk windows or a qualifying hard-rule event.
- Nodes performing critical consensus duties shall not be automatically diverted.
- Recovery from diversion shall require analyst review and key rotation.

### FR-05: Decoy Blockchain

- The system shall provide a separate permissioned blockchain environment.
- The decoy shall use synthetic accounts, transactions and balances.
- It shall reproduce the relevant structure and interfaces of the real blockchain.
- Writes performed in the decoy shall be reflected in subsequent reads within the same session.
- Each suspicious session shall receive an isolated copy-on-write data overlay.
- The decoy shall maintain its own hash-chained ledger.
- The decoy shall not have network routes to the real blockchain or its database.
- The decoy shall use independent cryptographic keys.
- The decoy shall be accessible only through the security gateway.

### FR-06: Real Blockchain Transaction Protection

The real blockchain shall independently validate every transaction.

The transaction integrity guardian shall check:

1. Digital signature validity.
2. Registered sender identity.
3. Nonce and sequence validity.
4. Channel and operation permissions.
5. Business invariants, such as non-negative balances and asset conservation.
6. Endorsement policies and applicable transaction limits.

The guardian shall reject invalid transactions independently of the gateway's risk assessment.

### FR-07: Forensic Evidence Collection

- The system shall record security events as canonical JSON.
- Each event shall be linked to the previous event using SHA-256 hashing.
- The gateway shall digitally sign forensic event batches using its Ed25519 key.
- The latest forensic chain head shall periodically be anchored to the real ledger.
- The system shall provide a verification mechanism to identify broken hash links.
- Event records shall be protected against unauthorised modification.

### FR-08: Security Dashboard

The system shall provide a dashboard displaying:

- Registered nodes and their current status.
- Current risk scores.
- Risk score history.
- Behavioural features contributing to risk.
- Applied security responses.
- Suspicious session information.
- Decoy blockchain interactions.
- Forensic incident timelines.
- Forensic integrity verification results.

### FR-09: Threat Intelligence and Policy Improvement

- Confirmed incidents shall generate candidate behavioural indicators.
- The system shall propose potential policy changes based on confirmed incidents.
- Candidate policies shall be tested against benign and attack simulation datasets.
- Policy changes shall require human approval.
- Approved policies shall be signed, versioned and reversible.
- Decoy activity shall never directly modify live security policies.

## 3. Non-Functional Requirements

Non-functional requirements define the expected quality, reliability and security of the system.

### NFR-01: Security

- All node identities shall be verified cryptographically.
- The decoy environment shall remain isolated from real blockchain infrastructure.
- Real blockchain transactions shall undergo independent validation.
- Forensic records shall support tamper detection.
- Private cryptographic keys shall not be shared between real and decoy environments.

### NFR-02: Performance

The prototype shall target:

- Gateway-added latency of less than 15 ms at the 95th percentile.
- Containment time below 5 seconds at the median for scripted attacks.
- Throughput reduction of less than 10% compared with the baseline blockchain under identical test workloads.

These are evaluation targets, not assumed results.

### NFR-03: Detection Quality

The prototype shall aim for:

- At least 90% attack-session detection rate.
- No more than 5% false-positive rate.
- Zero invalid or malicious transactions committed to the real ledger during the defined test scenarios.

### NFR-04: Reliability

- The gateway shall handle invalid requests without crashing.
- Risk calculations shall remain consistent across incoming requests.
- A failure in the decoy environment shall not expose the real blockchain.
- Security decisions and relevant events shall be recorded.

### NFR-05: Scalability

- The prototype shall support eight simulated nodes with different behavioural profiles.
- The architecture should allow additional nodes to be introduced without redesigning the entire system.
- Components shall be separated to support future scaling.

### NFR-06: Maintainability

- The system shall use modular components.
- Configuration and security policies shall be separated from application logic.
- Components shall have clear interfaces.
- Important security decisions shall be documented.
- Automated tests shall cover critical system behaviour.

### NFR-07: Usability

- The dashboard shall present security information clearly.
- Risk scores and response decisions shall be understandable.
- Forensic timelines shall make suspicious activities easy to investigate.
- Important errors shall provide meaningful information.

### NFR-08: Privacy and Ethical Use

- Only synthetic data shall be used in the decoy environment.
- Real personal and financial records shall not be copied into the decoy.
- The prototype shall be tested only in controlled environments.
- No external systems shall be targeted without explicit authorisation.

## 4. Prototype Constraints

The initial implementation shall:

1. Run on a single development machine using containerised services.
2. Use eight simulated blockchain nodes.
3. Use controlled benign and scripted attack workloads.
4. Use synthetic blockchain data.
5. Perform ten repeated evaluation runs with fixed random seeds.
6. Prioritise the stolen-credential reconnaissance scenario as the primary demonstration.

## 5. Acceptance Criteria

The prototype will be considered functionally complete when it demonstrates:

- Successful verification of legitimate node identities.
- Identification of suspicious behavioural changes.
- Dynamic risk-score calculation.
- All four adaptive response levels.
- Successful diversion of an eligible high-risk session.
- Interaction with the isolated decoy blockchain.
- Independent protection of the real blockchain.
- Tamper-evident forensic event recording.
- Incident visualisation through the dashboard.
- Measurable evaluation against a baseline configuration.

Performance and detection targets shall be measured and reported honestly during evaluation.

## 6. Conclusion

These requirements define the expected functionality and quality of PROJECT BLACKVEIL.

They provide the implementation boundaries and evaluation criteria needed to develop a working prototype that demonstrates adaptive deception, behavioural security assessment and independent blockchain transaction protection.
